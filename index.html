Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need to be technical?

No. We set up the Sheet with you on a video call and give you a simple product template. After that, daily billing is just scanning or typing item codes.

Where is my data stored?

In your own Google Sheet and Google Drive, under your Google account. We copy the system into your account during setup, so you own it from the start.

What do I need to start?

A Google account, a phone or computer with internet, and your product list. A barcode scanner and a label printer are optional.

Does it work with a barcode scanner?

Yes. A USB or Bluetooth scanner that types the code and presses Enter works directly. You can also type an item code or name, or take a photo of a barcode with your phone camera.

Does it send WhatsApp bills automatically?

It opens WhatsApp with the bill message and the PDF link already written for that customer, and you press Send. This keeps it free, with no WhatsApp API account needed.

How does GST work?

You set the GST % for each product. Bills show item-wise GST with a CGST and SGST split, and totals handle discount and round-off. It does not handle IGST for inter-state sales, e-invoicing or GST return filing, so keep using your accountant for those.

Can I bill without internet?

No. GSheetPOS runs on Google Sheets, so it needs an internet connection.

Which printers work?

Bills are 4 × 6 inch PDFs, which print on normal printers and on 4 × 6 label printers. They are not formatted for narrow 58 mm or 80 mm receipt rolls. Barcode labels are 50 × 25 mm.

How many bills and products can it handle?

The product list holds up to 1,000 items. GSheetPOS is designed for small and medium shops. Tell us your usual number of bills a day in the enquiry form and we’ll confirm it’s a good fit.

Can I see a demo first?

Yes. Send an enquiry and we’ll show you a live demo on a video call using your own products.

Does it support Tamil or other languages?

The screens are in English. Item names and addresses printed on bills, labels and shipping labels should be written in English letters, because other scripts do not print.

What happens after I pay?

We book a setup call, copy the system to your Google account, help you load your products, and make a test bill with you. Setup help and 30 days of support are included.
