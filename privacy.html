What we collect

When you fill the enquiry form we collect: your name, shop name, phone/WhatsApp number, email (optional), city, business type, GSTIN (optional), the approximate size of your business, your current software, whether you have a barcode scanner, what you need, your preferred call language and time, and any message you write.

Why we collect it

Only to contact you about your enquiry, to show a demo and to set up GSheetPOS for your shop. We do not sell your details or use them for unrelated advertising.

Where it is stored

Enquiries are saved in a private Google Sheet that only the owner of GSheetPOS can open, and a notification email is sent to the owner. Your phone number may be used to message you on WhatsApp, if you agreed to that on the form.

Your billing data

After you buy GSheetPOS, your sales, products and customers are stored in your own Google Sheet and Google Drive. We only see them if you share your screen or share the Sheet with us for support.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your enquiry details at any time by writing to you@example.com.
